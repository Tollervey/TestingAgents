KEY PATTERNS
================================================================================

POLLY RETRY POLICY (5 retries, ~30 minutes):
  Delays: 2s → 4s → 8s → 16s → 32s (+ 25% jitter)
  Total: ~62 seconds base + variance

HMAC-SHA256 WEBHOOK SIGNING:
  Generate: hex(HMAC-SHA256(payload, secret))
  Verify: Constant-time comparison (prevents timing attacks)
  Format: "X-Signature: sha256=<hex>"

NOTIFICATION STATUS FLOW:
  Created → Pending → [Success] → Delivered
                    → [Failure] → [Retry] → Pending (up to 5x)
                               → Failed
